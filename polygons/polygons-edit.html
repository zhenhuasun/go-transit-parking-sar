Draw Your Own Polygons — GO Parking Lots (2018–2026)

You can edit directly on this satellite map — no software to install, no account needed. The 6 draft boxes are already on the map.

  1. Edit a box: click the ✏️ Edit button at the top left of the map, then click a box and drag its white corner points; drag the middle of the box to move it as a whole. Click Save at the top left when done.
  2. Draw a new box: click the polygon button at the top left, click along the edge of the parking lot, and click the first point again to close it; a box will pop up asking for a name, e.g. Oakville GO — South Lot.
  3. Delete a box: click the 🗑️ Delete button at the top left, then click the box to delete, and click Save.
  4. Export: when done, click “Export GeoJSON” below, then “Copy” or “Download .geojson”, and send the file / text back to me — I will run SARpanel on the version you drew.

Your edits are saved automatically in this browser (localStorage) and will still be here next time you open this page. You can also draw on a phone, but the corner points are small — do fine editing on a computer and use the phone for rough checks only. Zoom to the maximum (z 18–19) before tracing, and cover only asphalt parking lots, not tracks, station buildings, roads or trees.

Send it back to me any one of these ways: 1) send the downloaded .geojson file directly; 2) click “Copy GeoJSON” and paste it to me; 3) no file needed — just tell me in chat which boxes you changed (e.g. “Oakville South moved south of the tracks”) and I will change them as you say. Do not draw the parking garage — it is excluded.
