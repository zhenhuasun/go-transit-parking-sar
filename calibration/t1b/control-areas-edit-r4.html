Draw Control Areas — GO Parking SAR (T1b, round 4: single Bronte control)

Round 4 uses one control for all 6 lots: the big unchanged warehouse roofs near Bronte GO. Draw three rooftops near Bronte — the existing Bronte control roof plus the two new ones you found. On export they are pooled into a single Bronte control (one MultiPolygon), so no single roof can sink the test. Each must be a surface with no parked cars, to check whether the weekend effect we found is real or just something the satellite does on weekends. The 6 parking lots are shown in blue for reference (you cannot edit them); the dashed grey circles are 500 m around each lot — a guide, not a rule.

  1. Draw 3 Bronte rooftops (each its own polygon). The existing Bronte roof is shown as an orange dashed outline — trace it as roof 1, then draw your two new roofs as roof 2 and roof 3. Trace each roof 10–15 m inside its edge, so no radar pixel touches the ground, loading dock, or truck court around the building. The popup shows the area as you draw.
  2. What counts: a large, flat, empty rooftop. What to avoid: parking lots, garages, roads, rail tracks, trees, water — and on the roof itself: HVAC units, solar panels, rooftop parking, or stored materials. Each roof must have been unchanged from 2018 to 2026 — no re-roof, no new units, no panels added (check the historical imagery, as you did for the others).
  3. Draw: click the polygon button at the top left of the map, click along the edge of the roof (inset from the real edge), and click the first point again to close it. Name it, e.g. Bronte GO — Control roof 1, then draw the next roof as roof 2.
  4. Start fresh: this page uses a new blank draft — your old Oakville/Clarkson polygons are not loaded here. If you open the old round-3 page by mistake, clear it and come back here.
  5. Edit / delete: use the ✏️ Edit and 🗑️ Delete buttons at the top left, then click Save.
  6. Export: when you have 3 Bronte roofs, click “Export GeoJSON” below, then “Copy” or “Download .geojson”, and send the file / text back to me. The export contains only the pooled Bronte control — nothing else.

Your edits are saved automatically in this browser (localStorage) and will still be here next time you open this page. You can also draw on a phone, but the corner points are small — do fine editing on a computer and use the phone for rough checks only. Zoom to the maximum (z 18–19) before tracing.

Send it back to me any one of these ways: 1) send the downloaded .geojson file directly; 2) click “Copy GeoJSON” and paste it to me.
