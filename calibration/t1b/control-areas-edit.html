Draw Control Areas — GO Parking SAR (T1b, round 3: multiple Oakville roofs)

Draw control areas — one polygon for Clarkson, one for Bronte, and one to three rooftops for Oakville. The Oakville rooftops will be pooled into a single Oakville control when you export (one MultiPolygon), so no single roof can sink the test on its own. Each must be a surface with no parked cars, to check whether the weekend effect we found is real or just something the satellite does on weekends. The 6 parking lots are shown in blue for reference (you cannot edit them); the dashed grey circles are 500 m around each lot — a guide, not a rule: Oakville roofs may lie farther out as long as they are nearby.

  1. Oakville — draw 1–3 rooftops (each its own polygon). Aim for a pooled total of at least ~10,000 m². Trace each roof 10–15 m inside its edge, so no radar pixel touches the ground, loading dock, or truck court around the building. The popup shows the area as you draw.
  2. What counts: a large, flat, empty rooftop. What to avoid: parking lots, garages, roads, rail tracks, trees, water — and on the roof itself: HVAC units, solar panels, rooftop parking, or stored materials. The roof must have been unchanged from 2018 to 2026 — no re-roof, no new units, no panels added (check the historical imagery).
  3. Draw: click the polygon button at the top left of the map, click along the edge of the roof (inset from the real edge), and click the first point again to close it. Name it, e.g. Oakville GO — Control roof 1, then draw the next roof as roof 2.
  4. Delete the old Oakville roof first: your previous Oakville control (7,232 m², the one that failed round 2) is still saved in this browser — use “Clear Oakville only” below before drawing the new roofs, or delete it with the 🗑️ Delete tool.
  5. Edit / delete: use the ✏️ Edit and 🗑️ Delete buttons at the top left, then click Save.
  6. Export: when Oakville has 1–3 roofs and Clarkson/Bronte have 1 polygon each, click “Export GeoJSON” below, then “Copy” or “Download .geojson”, and send the file / text back to me. The export pools the Oakville roofs into one control area.

Your edits are saved automatically in this browser (localStorage) and will still be here next time you open this page. You can also draw on a phone, but the corner points are small — do fine editing on a computer and use the phone for rough checks only. Zoom to the maximum (z 18–19) before tracing.

The green dashed outlines are the 20 largest roofs near Oakville GO from a map scan — click one for its area and distance, then trace the ones you pick. Red outlines are flagged do-not-pick (the roof that failed round 2, and the GO parking structure).
